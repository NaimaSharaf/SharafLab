Lab tools · Talks · Notes

Notes for every slide.

Open the PDF of your slides. Every slide becomes a picture with a notes box beside it, and your notes save automatically, either to a folder on your computer or inside this browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Keep a backup. These tools were vibe coded: built quickly with AI assistance. Notes kept “in this browser” disappear if you clear this site's data, so use a folder (Chrome or Edge) or download your notes for anything important.

    Get started

    1. Choose where your notes are kept: a folder on your computer (Chrome or Edge; for example a “Presentations” folder in Documents), or this browser (works everywhere).

    2. Click Add PDF and pick the PDF of your slides. Every slide appears as a picture with a notes box beside it.

    Notes save automatically. In a folder you get a copy of the PDF, the slide pictures, your notes (presentation.json) and a readable notes.md.
