Using it on your phone

  • Open this page on your phone and keep it in front while you talk. The screen stays on only while this page is showing.
  • Add it to your home screen for one-tap access: on iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen; on Android, open the browser menu, then Add to Home screen.
  • After time is up, the timer keeps counting how far over you are.
  • Use +1 min or −1 min if the session runs late or the chair asks you to shorten.
  • Your settings are remembered on this device.
