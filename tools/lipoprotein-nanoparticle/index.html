Lab tools · Figures · Nanoparticles

From one lipoprotein to a nanoparticle.

Design a single lipoprotein, choose how many copies and the color of their lipid tails, and the tool arranges them into a nanoparticle with the tails tucked into the core. Export the figure as SVG or PNG.

Check every result. These tools were vibe coded: built quickly with AI assistance and not independently validated. This is a schematic drawing, not a physical model, so it does not reflect real sizes, packing or aggregation numbers.

Your tail

Draw one tail with the end that joins the protein at the top (or set Tail points to match your file). Each tail is drawn from your file, so set Tails per lipoprotein to 1 if your file already shows the whole bundle. Add outline traces the edge of your tail in the tail outline color, using Tail outline width. Recoloring works on SVG files: the darkest color becomes the outline color, white stays white, and the rest become the tail color.

Protein head

Your protein

Use an image of just the protein, ideally with a transparent background, drawn with the side that joins the tail at the bottom. Use Head turn to adjust. Add outline traces the edge of your drawing in the head outline color, using Head outline width; it needs a transparent background. Recoloring works on SVG files: the darkest color becomes the outline, white stays white, and the rest become the head color.
