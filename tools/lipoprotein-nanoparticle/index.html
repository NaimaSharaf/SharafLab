Lab tools · Figures · Nanoparticles

From one lipoprotein to a nanoparticle.

Design a single lipoprotein, choose how many copies and the color of their lipid tails, and the tool arranges them into a nanoparticle with the tails tucked into the core. Export the figure as SVG or PNG.

Check every result. These tools were vibe coded: built quickly with AI assistance and not independently validated. This is a schematic drawing, not a physical model, so it does not reflect real sizes, packing or aggregation numbers.

Click a molecule to select it. Drag it to move it, drag the round handle to rotate it, drag the green diamond to swing the linker, or drag the orange square to turn just the protein domain (hold Shift on a handle to snap to 15°). Shift-drag a molecule to drag out a copy. Right-click any molecule, or the single lipoprotein and phospholipid, to change colors.

With Fit figure to width on, the preview and the export are scaled to exactly this width (height follows; the dashed frame is the export area) while every line width stays at the pt you set. The single lipoprotein and phospholipid are drawn and copied at the same scale, so they match the nanoparticle exactly. With it off, the SVG canvas is this size and molecules keep a fixed scale, so they may run past the edge. Export at scales the whole picture, line widths included: with Size 1 in and Export at 50%, you get a 0.5 in figure with every line half as thick. Set Document DPI to your Affinity document's DPI (File › Document Setup) so pastes land at the right size; use 72 for Illustrator.
