Select a residue in the alignment or in the 3D view to see where it is.

Conservation symbols follow Clustal conventions: * fully conserved; : strongly similar properties; . weakly similar properties. Columns with any gap get no symbol. Clustal coloring shades a residue when at least the chosen percent of the column shares its property group. Percent identity shades residues that match the most common residue in their column. Row-end numbers count residues, not gaps, as in Clustal output. Identity bars show the fraction of all sequences that carry the most common residue.

Files are processed in your browser and are never uploaded. The 3D view uses the open-source 3Dmol.js library, loaded from jsDelivr the first time you open a structure.
