Build a membrane figure.

Start from a membrane and add lipoproteins, transmembrane receptors, channels, ligands, arrows and labels. Drag to arrange them, remove what you don't need, and export the figure as SVG or PNG.

Check every result. These tools were vibe coded: built quickly with AI assistance and not independently validated. This is a schematic drawing, not a physical model, so it does not reflect real sizes or membrane thickness.

Tracing image

Figure
Zoom with + − (or pinch / ⌘/Ctrl-scroll); scroll or Space-drag to move around; 0 fits the figure. Drag the teal squares around a selected piece to resize it: the opposite side stays put (Alt/Option resizes from the centre, Shift keeps proportions). Double-click any part (a head, the tails, a domain, the membrane) to edit it: drag the orange squares to resize (corners change width and height; hold Shift to keep proportions), drag the orange dots to reshape corners, add shapes, mirror, copy and fuse them. Drag to move (hold Shift while dragging to keep it level or straight up and down). Drag the round handle to rotate (hold Shift for 30° steps). Alt/Option-drag makes a copy. Delete removes, arrow keys nudge.
Resize the whole figure (outlines and lines stay in pt; lipid tails scale)

At 100% one drawing unit is one point, so line widths are in pt. Your figure and library are kept in this browser between visits; use Save session to keep them in a file you can open again.
