Lab tools.

Small browser-based utilities built by the Sharaf Lab. Files are processed on your own device and are never uploaded.

Check every result. These tools were vibe coded: built quickly with AI assistance and not independently validated. Compare their output with your own data and standard software before using it in figures, analyses or publications.

Browser tools

Pick a tool.

Sequence · Domains · Topology · 3D

From sequence to topology

Open a DSSP-processed CIF to see secondary structure residue by residue, find domains, draw a topology diagram and view the chain in 3D.

→ Alignment · Conservation · 3D

Multiple sequence alignment viewer

Color Clustal, FASTA or Stockholm alignments by conservation, map them onto one or more 3D structures, mark binding sites and ligands, and export SVG figures.

→ Structure · Micelles · 3D

Pack proteins into micelles

Give a protein a hydrophobic tail, choose how many copies, and watch them assemble into micelles, rods or bilayers, with the packing parameter and shape of each assembly.

→ Figures · Lipoproteins · Nanoparticles

Lipoprotein nanoparticle builder

Draw one lipoprotein, choose the tail color and how many copies, and arrange them into a nanoparticle figure with the tails in the core. Export as SVG or PNG.

→ Chemistry · Structures · Figures

Structure tiles

Add molecules by CAS number, name or SMILES and tile their 2D structures across a figure with the same font, bond length and 1 pt lines. Copy as SVG.

→ PDF · Notes

Extend PDF for notes

Add blank space to every page of a paper or slide deck so you have room to annotate. The original content is never changed.

→ Talks · Phone

Presentation timer

A big, color-changing countdown for your phone that keeps the screen on while you present and keeps counting if you run over.
