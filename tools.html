Secondary structure, residue by residue.

Open a DSSP-annotated CIF or mmCIF file to draw helices, strands, and loops above the amino acid sequence, adjust the figure, and download it as an SVG.

File data used



Paste CIF or a structure table
Open a DSSP-annotated .cif or .mmcif file, or paste its contents below. CIF files with a polymer sequence scheme and supported structure ranges also work. The viewer reads existing annotations; it does not calculate DSSP from atomic coordinates. The original whitespace-separated table format is also supported. Raw PDB and classic fixed-width DSSP files are not supported.


Element labels use CIF identifiers: α1 means alpha helix 1; β1 · A means strand 1 in sheet A. Hover over a label for the full identifier. Edited assignments are marked “Edited”. Coils represent helices: α-helices (H) and 3₁₀ / π helices (G, I) use separate colors; arrows represent E strands and B bridges. Other assignments appear as lines. Wrapped segments continue onto the next row; arrows point toward the C terminus. This is a sequence schematic, not a 3D structure.
Files are processed in your browser. No upload service or external libraries.
